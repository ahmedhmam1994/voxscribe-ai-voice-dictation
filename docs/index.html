Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Built to feel invisible: it stays out of your way until you need it. Read the full tutorial for every feature, step by step.

Hold-to-talk hotkey

Hold F9 anywhere on your system: any app, any window. Release to transcribe, no toggling or mode switching.

Types directly, not clipboard

Text is typed straight into the focused field, so your clipboard contents are never touched or overwritten.

100% local transcription

Speech-to-text runs on your own PC, on the CPU or an NVIDIA graphics card. Your voice audio never leaves your computer.

Free filler-word cleanup

Strips "um", "uh", "like", "you know" and fixes spacing and capitalization. Rule-based, no paid AI API.

Spoken commands and undo

Say "period" or "new line" for symbols, "scratch that" to take back what you just typed, and "capitalize that" or "replace X with Y" to change it.

Audio and video to captions

Import a file or a whole folder and get an .srt next to each one: short two line cards, timed from the words and cut at pauses, and never overwriting an existing file.

Android app

The same offline dictation as a floating button in any Android app. See the Android app.

Meeting notes

Start meeting records your microphone and what the PC plays, removes the echo, and writes a markdown note with action items, decisions and questions into a folder you choose, such as your Obsidian vault.

Term packs for medicine, law and business

Words like MRI, metformin, habeas corpus and EBITDA are written correctly with any speech model. Import your own list as a plain text file.

Teach VoxScribe your voice

Read a short script and VoxScribe finds the words it mishears in your voice or accent, then fixes them from then on. Recordings stay on your PC.

Spoken formats

"One twenty over eighty" becomes 120/80, "five milligrams twice daily" becomes 5 mg BID, "section twelve subsection b" becomes section 12(b).

More speech models, your choice

Cohere Transcribe for Arabic that keeps your dialect, Parakeet for fast, accurate English, Moonshine Arabic, and optional NVIDIA GPU. Each is downloaded only if you pick it.

Notes inbox key

Hold a key and speak, and the words are saved as a timestamped line in a markdown file instead of being typed.

Read it back, and unsure words

Hear each dictation in Windows' own voice, and see the words the model was unsure about underlined. Both are off until you turn them on.

Try harder, modes and snippets

Press F8 to rerun a recording through a bigger model, switch between Chat, Email, Code and Notes, and expand short phrases into full text with Pro.

Get started

Install in under a minute

No admin rights required.

  1. Download the installer from the Releases page
  2. Run VoxScribe-Setup.exe and follow the prompts
  3. On first launch, VoxScribe downloads the speech model (a few hundred MB). This needs internet once; after that, transcription is fully offline
  4. Hold F9 anywhere and start talking

Prefer a package manager? With winget: winget install ahmedhmam1994.VoxScribe. Or with Scoop: scoop bucket add voxscribe https://github.com/ahmedhmam1994/scoop-voxscribe then scoop install voxscribe

Windows SmartScreen note: VoxScribe is a new, unsigned app, and because it hooks the keyboard (for the hotkey and to type text), Windows may show a "Windows protected your PC" warning on first run. Click More info → Run anyway to proceed. This is expected for unsigned indie software, not a sign of a problem. The full source is public on GitHub if you want to verify it yourself. FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

The core app is, forever, under the MIT license: no subscription, no account required. There's an optional one-time $14 Pro upgrade for snippets and larger Whisper models, but dictation itself is fully free.

Does it need an internet connection?

Only once, to download the speech-to-text model on first launch. After that, transcription works fully offline. Your voice audio is never uploaded anywhere.

Which languages can I dictate in?

Windows dictates in about 28 languages plus auto-detect, including Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Arabic, Hindi, Chinese, Japanese and Korean. Android currently offers English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Turkish and Arabic, plus auto-detect. Smaller models make more mistakes outside English, so on Windows try the Small model or larger for other languages.

What platforms does it support?

Windows 10 and 11, and Android 8 or newer. On Android a small floating button works in any app; download the APK from the releases page, since it is not on the Play Store yet.

Does it work with any microphone?

Yes. VoxScribe auto-detects your default input device (including Bluetooth headsets) and adapts to its native sample rate.

Why does Windows show a security warning when I install it?

VoxScribe is free and not yet code-signed, so Windows asks you to confirm once: click More info, then Run anyway. Installing with winget or Scoop usually skips it. The install warning guide shows how to check the file first and exactly what the installer changes.

How accurate is the transcription?

It uses faster-whisper's "small" model, which offers a solid balance of speed and accuracy on CPU for everyday dictation.

I lost my Pro license key. Can I get it back?

Yes. Open a GitHub issue with the name or email you used to buy it, and I'll look it up and send it back to you.
