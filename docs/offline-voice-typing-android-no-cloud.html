Android Voice Typing That Never Leaves Your Phone

Most voice typing on Android depends on an online speech service. VoxScribe for Android transcribes on the phone itself, with the speech model bundled in the app, so it works in airplane mode and your voice is never uploaded.

Free & open source (MIT) · Android 8 or newer · works with any keyboard

How it works

A small floating button appears above your keyboard whenever you tap a text box, in any app. Hold it or tap it, talk, and the words are typed into the box. It runs OpenAI's Whisper on the phone through sherpa-onnx, and the model is part of the roughly 115 MB download, so there is no second download and no account.

What stays on the phone

Your audio is transcribed on the device and discarded. The app only uses the internet for a check for new versions, an anonymous once-a-day usage ping that can be turned off in Settings, and Pro activation if you buy it. None of that includes your audio or your text. The full details are in the privacy summary.

Handy extras

  • Spoken commands such as "period", "new line", "scratch that" and "replace X with Y".
  • Email, web address and number boxes get the text in the form they expect, so "sam at gmail dot com" becomes sam@gmail.com.
  • File transcription: turn audio and video files into text or .srt captions, on the phone.
  • It stays out of the way: drag the button to the bottom of the screen to hide it for 10 minutes, and it never shows in password or PIN boxes.
Install: the app is an APK on GitHub Releases, not on the Play Store. Add the repository to Obtainium to get updates automatically. Step by step instructions are on the Android guide.

Frequently asked questions

Does VoxScribe for Android work offline?

Yes. The speech model is bundled inside the app, so there is nothing to download and it works in airplane mode. Your voice is transcribed on the phone.

Does it replace my keyboard?

No. It works with whichever keyboard you already use. A small floating button appears when you tap a text box, and you hold it or tap it to talk.

Why does it need the Accessibility permission?

Android only lets an app type into other apps through an Accessibility service. VoxScribe uses it only to put the dictated text into the box you are focused on, and the button never appears in password or PIN fields.

Is it free?

Yes. Dictation is free and open source under the MIT license, with no ads. An optional one-time Pro upgrade unlocks extras such as per-app profiles and code dictation.
